Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 24, 2026

Alabama Beach Flag ("the App") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting any information you provide while using the application.

Information We Collect

Alabama Beach Flag does not require users to create an account and does not collect personally identifiable information such as names, addresses, phone numbers, or payment information.

Location Information

The App may use location services if permission is granted by the user. Location information is used solely to improve the user experience and is not stored, sold, or shared by the developer.

Usage Data

The App may receive publicly available weather, beach condition, and safety information from third-party services in order to provide current beach conditions and safety alerts.

Third-Party Services

Alabama Beach Flag may utilize third-party data providers, including weather and public safety services, to display current information. These providers may have their own privacy policies and terms.

Data Sharing

Alabama Beach Flag does not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share personal information with third parties.

Children's Privacy

The App is intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
