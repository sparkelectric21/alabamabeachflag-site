Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2, 2026

Alabama Beach Flag ("the App") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your information. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app collects and how it is used.

Information We Collect

Alabama Beach Flag does not require an account and does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or payment information. The app does not use analytics, advertising SDKs, or user tracking.

Location Information

Alabama Beach Flag does not collect, store, or transmit your device's location. Beach information is displayed based on the beach you select within the app.

Device Storage

Your selected favorite beach is stored locally on your device to remember your preference and support Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets. This information never leaves your device.

Public Information

The app retrieves publicly available beach flag, weather, water quality, marine, and safety information from official public sources to display current beach conditions and related safety information.

Third-Party Services

Alabama Beach Flag retrieves official public information through its secure backend, which aggregates publicly available information from trusted government agencies and local authorities including the National Weather Service, the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA), the Alabama Department of Environmental Management (ADEM), and local beach authorities.

Data Sharing

Alabama Beach Flag does not sell, rent, trade, or share personal information because no personal information is collected.

Children's Privacy

The App is intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
