Beach-specific planning

Beach Activity & Event Impact

The app may highlight a scheduled activity happening at—or directly affecting—the exact supported beach you are viewing, including major access-impacting events, cleanups, wildlife and conservation activities, educational programs, and community events. It is not a complete regional events calendar and does not report live attendance, crowd size, or parking availability.

Exact beach matches

Events appear only after an official venue, exact address, or approved location reference is matched to an existing beach.

Visit impact, carefully worded

High-impact events may note that parking, traffic, beach space, or access could be affected. Confirm current details with the organizer.

Submit an event

Authorized organizers may email support@alabamabeachflag.com with their contact information, exact supported beach, date and time, meeting location, organizer, and official HTTPS information link. Please confirm that you are authorized to provide the facts and consent to their display. Every submission enters pending review, receives spam screening, and can never publish directly.
