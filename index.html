Beach-specific planning

Beach Activity & Event Impact

The app can highlight a scheduled activity happening at the exact beach you are viewing—such as a cleanup, wildlife program, race, festival, or access-impacting event. It is not a general area events calendar and does not claim live crowds or parking availability.

Exact beach matches

Events appear only after an official venue, exact address, or approved location reference is matched to an existing beach.

Visit impact, carefully worded

High-impact events may note that parking, traffic, beach space, or access could be affected. Confirm current details with the organizer.

Submit an event

Organizers may email support@alabamabeachflag.com with the exact beach, date, venue, and official HTTPS link. Submission does not guarantee publication.
